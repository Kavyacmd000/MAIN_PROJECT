# 🛋️ IKEA Global Product Catalog Analysis Dashboard

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This final Data Analytics project focuses on analyzing IKEA’s global product catalog using Python for data cleaning and exploratory analysis, along with Power BI for dashboard creation and business visualization.

The project provides insights into product categories, pricing patterns, customer ratings, and overall product distribution using interactive visual analytics.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses like IKEA manage thousands of products across multiple categories and price ranges.

This project helps analyze:
- Product category distribution
- Customer rating patterns
- Product pricing behavior
- High-demand product categories
- Price vs rating relationships
- Product outliers and premium items

# 🔍 Key Insights Generated

✔ Storage and kitchen categories contain the highest number of products  
✔ Most IKEA products belong to affordable and medium price ranges  
✔ Customer ratings are generally high (mostly between 4 and 5)  
✔ Expensive products do not always receive higher ratings  
✔ Premium products appear as outliers with lower popularity  

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus more on high-demand categories such as storage and kitchen products
- Improve marketing strategies for medium-priced products
- Analyze premium product performance further
- Continue maintaining product quality standards
- Use customer feedback to improve lower-rated products
